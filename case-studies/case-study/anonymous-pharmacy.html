The challenge

Every creative had to clear two approval queues. Six-day build cycles became multi-week ordeals when revisions came in. For a regulated category like pharma, the cost of a miss isn't just time — different product categories require different legal disclaimers, and manually scaling that creates real legal exposure if any single asset slips through.

The solution

DaVinci Commerce's compliance engine collapsed both regimes into one AI pass. The system checks creative against both guideline sets simultaneously, returns a score and specific fixes in one click, and lets internal teams build and approve without a vendor hand-off or a review queue. Disclaimer logic by product category is encoded once and applied automatically — the compliance bar is enforced before the asset leaves the studio.

Results

  • Under 1 day to production-ready creative.
  • 100% first-pass approval across all campaigns.
  • External vendor dependency fully eliminated.
