How Diageo runs commerce media at SKU level.

The challenge

Diageo runs SKU-level creative across more than ten spirit brands and dozens of markets — each with its own retailer relationships, promotional calendars, and compliance requirements.

Under the legacy model, briefs went to agencies. Agencies came back with revisions. By the time creative cleared compliance, the promotional window had already closed. And because everything ran at brand level rather than SKU level, there was no way to know what was actually resonating, or with whom.

The solution

DaVinci Commerce rebuilt the pipeline end-to-end. Live product feeds replaced manual asset hand-offs. AI-powered compliance checking against retailer ad-spec libraries replaced the revision loop. SKU-matched creative auto-mapped to retailer, audience, and promotional window without an agency in the middle.

First-pass approval became the standard, not the exception. The team moved from operating like a creative production line to operating like a media-optimization team.

Results

  • 3.5x faster launches — from 15+ days to under 24 hours.
  • 2x ROAS increase over prior campaigns.
  • 80% increase in speed-to-market across all channels.
  • $6.6M in additional annual revenue from commerce-media activation — a 15x return on platform fee in three months.
  • 100% first-pass approval across all creative.
