The standards, in plain language

Three open protocols do the work.

Agentic commerce is not magic. It runs on a set of standards that describe how agents read catalogs, transact, and call your brand. Here is what each one does.

ACP

Agentic Commerce Protocol

The standard for how AI agents enable contextual discovery and transactions. It lets an agent move from a recommendation to a real purchase, handing the shopper to your preferred checkout instead of guessing.

UCP

Universal Commerce Protocol

The standard for how your catalog is read across agents. It describes your products, attributes, and availability, so every assistant understands them the same way.

MCP

Model Context Protocol

The standard for how your Answer Agent is called as a tool. It is the connection a shopper opens when they invoke your brand, so the right Storefront responds with your content.
