Build me a routine for combination skin with hormonal breakouts.

For combination skin with breakouts, most routines focus on a few common steps. A gentle cleanser morning and night helps, and many people use a niacinamide or salicylic acid product for oil and blemishes:

  • Cleanse twice a day with a gentle, non-stripping wash.
  • Salicylic acid or benzoyl peroxide can help with breakouts.
  • Use a lightweight moisturizer, heavier in winter.
  • Daily SPF is important when using actives.
  • Patch test new products and introduce one at a time.

If you are pregnant or have sensitivities, speak to a dermatologist before adding actives.
