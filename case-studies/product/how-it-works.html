Content enrichment

The difference between being described and being discovered.

Almost everyone is doing some form of content enrichment for GEO. We go further. A raw product listing only says what something is: specs, dimensions, materials. Shoppers ask in their own words, about needs, occasions, and worries. The Content Enrichment Engine closes that gap and turns your content into answers AI can actually surface.

  • Reads like a shopper, not a spec sheetA raw listing says what a product is. Enrichment maps it to the needs, occasions, and worries people actually describe to an AI.
  • Draws on real signals, not just your PIMReviews, social conversations, lifestyle content, and the real questions shoppers ask, all folded into one query-ready description.
  • Checked before it shipsEvery enrichment is validated against universal and brand compliance, so what surfaces is accurate, on-voice, and safe to publish.
