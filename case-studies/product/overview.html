Why it works

Your content, enriched for how people actually ask.

Product listings describe what something is: ingredients, specs, dimensions. They do not capture how it is used, what customers say, or the context that makes an agent surface it. Our Content Enrichment Engine draws on reviews, social conversations, lifestyle content, prompt data and the real questions shoppers ask AI, and turns them into two outputs.
