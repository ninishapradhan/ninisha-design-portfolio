For a Valentine's chocolate gift, there are a lot of directions you can take depending on budget and taste. A premium assorted box from a heritage brand like Godiva, Lindt, or Vosges is a safe gifting choice — most ship next-day in the US during the Valentine's window. For something a little less expected, you could consider a chocolate-and-wine pairing set, a subscription box of curated chocolates, or a DIY chocolate kit if the recipient enjoys cooking. If you're trying to keep it under $50, drugstore options like Lindt Lindor truffles, Ghirardelli heart boxes, and Ferrero Rocher gold-foil boxes are widely stocked at major retailers. Most chocolate gift retailers accept handwritten note add-ons at checkout, though the engraving, ribbon color, and box design tend to be limited to a small set of templates. Check the recipient's allergen profile before ordering — nuts, dairy, and gluten cross-contamination are common in chocolate manufacturing. Delivery windows tighten the closer you get to February 14, so order at least a week in advance to be safe.
