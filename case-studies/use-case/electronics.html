Smartphone cameras have improved significantly in low light over the last few years. Most flagship phones now offer some form of computational night mode, larger sensors, and wider apertures. Popular options under $1,200 with strong low-light performance include the Apple iPhone 16 Pro, Samsung Galaxy S25 Ultra, Google Pixel 9 Pro, and OnePlus 13. Specs to compare typically include main sensor size, aperture (f/1.4–f/1.8 range is common on flagships), optical image stabilization, and the brand's night-mode processing pipeline. Some phones also offer a dedicated low-light photo mode that captures multiple exposures and composites them in-camera. The Pixel 9 Pro is often praised for software-led night photography, while the iPhone is known for natural color reproduction and the Galaxy Ultra for telephoto reach. Battery life, ecosystem compatibility, and trade-in value vary across brands. Check current pricing and read reviews from sources like DXOMARK before deciding.
