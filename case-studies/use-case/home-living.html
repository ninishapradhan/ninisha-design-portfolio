Japandi style is a popular interior design movement that blends Japanese minimalism with Scandinavian warmth. For a small living room, look for low-profile sofas in light or mid-tone woods like ash, oak, or walnut, paired with natural-fibre textiles in warm tones — oat, clay, sand. Popular Japandi-friendly sofas in this size range include low-arm or armless silhouettes around 78–84 inches, with linen or boucle upholstery. A few things to keep in mind: measure your doorway and the longest wall before deciding, and watch out for made-to-order lead times that can stretch to 8–12 weeks.
