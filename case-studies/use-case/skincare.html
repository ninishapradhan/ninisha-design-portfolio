Skincare for combination skin with hormonal breakouts is a broad category. A general routine usually has a gentle cleanser, a targeted treatment serum, a hydrating moisturizer, and daily SPF. Common ingredient categories that show up in routines for breakout-prone skin include niacinamide for inflammation, salicylic acid as a chemical exfoliant, and retinoids for cell turnover. Some shoppers also reach for benzoyl peroxide spot treatments. Patch test new actives for 48 hours before adding them to a routine, and introduce one active at a time so you can identify what your skin is responding to. If you are pregnant, on prescription topicals, or have known sensitivities, you should speak with a dermatologist before adding actives like retinoids or higher-strength acids to your routine. Daily SPF is non-negotiable when using exfoliating actives.
